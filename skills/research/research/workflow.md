# research workflow

Deep-dive loop for one question (raw/research workspace via
`setup/new-research.sh` when multi-session):

1. Frame the question; check the wiki FIRST (it may already hold the answer).
2. wigolo (cache-first) + web; VM tools for anything target-adjacent.
3. Distill: technique pages to `wiki/`, tells to `scripts/tells.json`,
   per-session state under `raw/research/<project>/` (public targets only).
